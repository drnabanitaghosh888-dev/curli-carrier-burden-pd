# Curli Carrier Burden Formula (Template)

CurliCarrierBurden_s = sum_i abundance_s,i * CurliCandidateConfidence_i

Where:
- s is sample
- i is candidate taxon
- abundance_s,i is processed-table abundance
- CurliCandidateConfidence_i is confidence weight from Phase 1-derived taxa catalog

Important: this is a proxy only and not proof of curli expression or operon integrity.

## Marker reliability note

Reliable Phase 1 markers: csgD, csgE, csgF, csgG.

Cautious markers: csgA, csgB, csgC.

Interpretation caveats:
- csgA and csgB showed cross-reactivity and should preferably be interpreted as a combined csgAB structural/nucleator module.
- csgC and csgE showed partial cross-reactivity and require cautious interpretation.
- CurliCarrierBurden is a processed-table proxy only.
- Targeted raw-read csg screening is required later to validate gene-level and operon-level claims.
