# PHASE2E Interpretation After Wallen Discovery

- Wallen is a discovery cohort result, not a final causal conclusion.
- The unadjusted association attenuated after adjustment for age, sex, and BMI.
- Replication should prioritize direction, effect size, and confidence intervals over binary significance calls.
- Replication failure would weaken curli-specific interpretation, but may still be compatible with broader Enterobacteriaceae dysbiosis hypotheses.
- Raw-read csg validation remains required before any gene-level or operon-level claim.
- Run-accession mapping remains a gating dependency for future raw-read follow-up.
